# Figure descriptions

All PNGs are generated by `src/make_figures.py` at 300 dpi. Numbering here identifies package files and can be aligned with the final manuscript later. AI Occupational Exposure is abbreviated AIOE. ARI is adjusted Rand index. PC1 and PC2 are the first two principal components.

## 01_k_selection.png

Candidate cluster diagnostics for domain-weighted K-means. A shows within-cluster squared-distance sums (Inertia), B shows mean silhouette scores for k = 2–15, and C shows mean and minimum pairwise ARI for k = 2–9. ARI summarizes 190 pairs among 20 seeds (0–19), with `n_init=10` in each fit. Seed 42 supplies the reference inertia and silhouette values.

## 02_pca_k4.png

Two-dimensional PCA projection of the 878 occupations in the k = 4 solution. Each point is an occupation; colors show cluster membership, and black X symbols show projected cluster means. The PCA input is the actual domain-weighted 187-feature matrix. PC1 and PC2 explain approximately 36.44% and 11.07% of variance. Clustering and its diagnostic metrics are computed in the full feature space, not in this projection.

## 03_communication_profiles.png

Four clusters' mean scores for the common set of 14 communication-related features. Values are cluster means of feature z-scores before domain weighting, standardized across 878 occupations. Red and blue indicate positive and negative relative values. Horizontal separators organize contact/service, understanding/coordination, and support features for interpretation. These blocks are not validated subscales or classification thresholds. The same features are shown for all four clusters.

## 04_mean_aioe.png

Mean general, language-modeling, and image-generation AIOE by cluster. Each occupation receives equal weight. Each exposure measure is separately standardized against the same 910 calculable occupations using a sample standard deviation. Cells show three-decimal means, and n gives the number of occupations in each cluster. Cross-measure comparisons concern relative positions within different exposure distributions, not absolute technological effects.

## 05_exposure_distributions.png

Occupational exposure distributions for Public-facing Service and Support (n = 215) and Interpersonal Support and Coordination (n = 242). Panels A–C show general, language-modeling, and image-generation AIOE. Boxes span Q1–Q3, internal lines mark medians, and whiskers reach the furthest observed values within 1.5 IQR. Quantiles use linear interpolation. Points outside whiskers remain included in all summaries. The dashed vertical line is the 910-occupation reference mean of zero.

## 06_initialization_contrasts.png

Mean exposure differences across 20 initializations. Each point subtracts the Public-facing Service and Support mean from the Interpersonal Support and Coordination mean for the same fit. Panels A–C show general, language-modeling, and image-generation AIOE. Dashed horizontal lines show the seed-42 reference difference. All fits use `n_init=10`. Cluster correspondence is determined by maximum membership overlap without using AIOE. Positive differences occur in all 20 runs. Ranges reflect initialization variability, not confidence intervals.
